FAQs

Q: How does the CloudBooks subscription system work?

A: Once you subscribe, you get full and unlimited access to all the books in the library for the duration of your subscription.

Q: Can I download books and read them offline?

A: Yes, the app allows you to download books into your account to read at any time.

Q: How can I cancel my subscription?

A: You can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings (user dashboard), and the books you need will remain available to you until the end of the current subscription period.

Q: Are local payment methods available?

A: Yes, we support payment via “Fawry” and electronic wallets to facilitate subscription for students.
